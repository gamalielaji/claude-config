PART F — what actually drives the fee/LVR ratio (controlled sweeps)
   Sweeps below vary ONE input at a time, jumps disabled, to isolate the
   diffusive regime. Result: the ratio scales with gamma/sigma and is
   INDEPENDENT of bin step and of hold time. A tempting closed form
   'ratio = 2*gamma/delta' is refuted by sweep D — see README.md App.A3.
==============================================================================
  A) vary fee rate gamma  -> ratio is LINEAR in gamma
     gamma=  0.5%  fees  1.314%  inv  -2.410%  ratio   0.55  crossings  157.7
     gamma=  1.0%  fees  2.628%  inv  -2.410%  ratio   1.09  crossings  157.7
     gamma=  2.0%  fees  5.256%  inv  -2.410%  ratio   2.18  crossings  157.7
     gamma=  4.0%  fees 10.513%  inv  -2.410%  ratio   4.36  crossings  157.7
  B) vary sigma_daily     -> ratio falls roughly as 1/sigma
     sigma=  30%   fees  2.472%  inv  -0.562%  ratio   4.40  crossings   74.2
     sigma=  60%   fees  5.256%  inv  -2.410%  ratio   2.18  crossings  157.7
     sigma= 120%   fees 10.132%  inv  -9.434%  ratio   1.07  crossings  304.0
  C) vary hold time       -> ratio ~invariant (both legs grow with T)
     T=   60min  fees  1.211%  inv  -0.580%  ratio   2.09  crossings   36.3
     T=  240min  fees  5.256%  inv  -2.410%  ratio   2.18  crossings  157.7
     T= 1440min  fees 28.000%  inv -14.229%  ratio   1.97  crossings  840.0
  D) vary bin step at FIXED range depth -> ratio INVARIANT (refutes 2g/d)
     step=  25bps nbins= 240 delta=0.00250  ratio   2.18   ('2g/d' would predict  16.02)
     step=  50bps nbins= 120 delta=0.00499  ratio   2.18   ('2g/d' would predict   8.02)
     step= 100bps nbins=  60 delta=0.00995  ratio   2.18   ('2g/d' would predict   4.02)
     step= 200bps nbins=  30 delta=0.01980  ratio   2.18   ('2g/d' would predict   2.02)

  Fitting ratio = k * gamma / sigma_daily on the sweeps above gives k ~ 65.
  CAUTION: k is NOT universal. It is set by how often the pool re-prices
  (this model observes the path once per minute). Estimate your own k from
  realised fills:  k = fees_earned * sigma_daily / (gamma * capital * ratio).
  The SHAPE (ratio proportional to gamma/sigma) is the transferable part.

  Same configs WITH jumps re-enabled (bin_step=100bps):
  jumps/day= 0.0 rug/day=0.00  fees  5.173%  inv  -2.602%  ratio   1.99  mean PnL +2.571%
  jumps/day= 6.0 rug/day=0.00  fees  5.390%  inv  -2.911%  ratio   1.85  mean PnL +2.479%
  jumps/day= 6.0 rug/day=0.25  fees  5.346%  inv  -4.816%  ratio   1.11  mean PnL +0.529%
  jumps/day=12.0 rug/day=0.50  fees  5.449%  inv  -6.676%  ratio   0.82  mean PnL -1.227%

  -> The diffusive ratio is the edge. Jump intensity is what eats it.
     Pool selection = maximising diffusive vol per unit of jump vol.
